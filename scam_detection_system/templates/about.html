How it works

ScamCheck uses a machine learning model that learned from thousands of real text messages that people had already labelled as legitimate or spam/scam.

From message to result

  1. Cleaning. The text is converted to lowercase. Links, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and money amounts are replaced by placeholder words (for example every link becomes "urltoken"), because their presence matters more than their exact value. Links are never opened.
  2. TF-IDF. The words and word pairs are turned into numbers. Words that are common in this message but rare in other messages get the highest weight.
  3. Prediction. The model calculates the probability that the message is a scam. At 50% or more it is labelled "Potential Scam".
  4. Warning indicators. Separate, simple pattern checks point out common scam tactics (links, urgency, prizes, requests for a PIN...). They help explain the message but do not change the model's prediction.

Understanding your result

  • Potential Scam - the message resembles scam messages the model has seen. Verify the sender through an official channel before acting.
  • No obvious scam patterns detected - the model did not recognise scam patterns. This is not a guarantee that the message is safe.
  • Confidence - the model's estimated probability for its prediction. Low confidence means the model is unsure.

Limitations

  • Machine learning predictions are not always correct.
  • New scam techniques may not be recognised.
  • The model learned from an English SMS dataset (the UCI SMS Spam Collection) that mostly contains UK and Singapore messages and labels spam, which is broader than scams. It may not recognise local mobile-money scams or messages in other languages.
  • A legitimate message can be incorrectly flagged, and a scam can be classified as legitimate.
  • The system is a detection aid and should not be treated as a guarantee of safety.

Privacy

Analysed messages are stored so the system can be monitored and improved. Phone numbers and e-mail addresses are replaced with placeholders before saving, and no information about you (such as your IP address) is stored. Avoid submitting passwords or other private details.
