 GHION ERP — Manufacturing & Accounting System

A web-based ERP built for **GHION INVESTMENTS AND ENTERPRISE LTD**, a tissue and
paper-products manufacturer in Uganda. It replaces Excel-based record keeping with a
single, auditable system covering the factory floor to the financial statements.

 Key features
- Production — production runs linked to individually traceable jumbo batches,
  estimated running balances (no heavy scale needed), waste tracking
- Confidential purchase workflow — the Accountant records receipts only;
  purchase prices are entered by the Director/Consultant and are hidden from the
  Accountant at the query level (not just the UI)
- Sales & receivables — multi-line invoices, credit sales, customer ledgers,
  part-payments, aging
- Inventory — batch register, stock movement ledger, stocktakes with
  variance preservation, finished-goods tracking
- Payroll — employee-specific PAYE/NSSF applicability, configurable statutory
  rates, payslips
- Accounting — auto-generated journal entries from sales, trial balance,
  chart of accounts
- Audit trail — who, when, what changed, old to new values, IP address
- Role-based access — three roles: Accountant, Director, Consultant



Designed for offline-first operation with a sync queue and idempotent transaction
IDs; deployable on a local LAN server (XAMPP) with remote access.
